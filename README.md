# Orbit · Dhananjay Singh Madaik

A cinematic GIS and Earth-observation portfolio: a 3D Earth that flies to each study area
(Himachal Pradesh, Kinnaur, Rome, Valencia, Cornwall, Manchester), shows the place, the work and the maps,
and opens each full case study.

Live: https://dhananjaysmadaik.github.io/orbit/ · Classic version: https://dhananjaysmadaik.github.io/

## What is in here

- `index.html`, `assets/` – the published site (no outside scripts, fonts or trackers; strict Content Security Policy)
- `maps/`, `thumbs/`, `reports/` – full-size maps, light previews and reports (metadata removed)
- `src/` – sources: `build.py` assembles the page, `globe.js` is the 3D Earth and satellite, `app.js` the scroll journey
- `tools/earth-engine-true-colour.js` – Google Earth Engine script for true-colour Sentinel-2 views of the study areas
